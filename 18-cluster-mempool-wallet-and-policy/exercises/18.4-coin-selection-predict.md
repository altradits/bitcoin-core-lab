# Stage 18 Exercise 18.4: Predict coin selection, then ask the wallet

**Output:** `my-work/18-cluster-mempool-wallet-and-policy/coinsel.env` and journal.
**Checker:** `./18-cluster-mempool-wallet-and-policy/check_18.sh coinsel` (asks your regtest node)

Read `src/wallet/coinselection.h` (the comments above each algorithm) and `doc/release-notes`
entries mentioning BnB/CoinGrinder before you start.

## Step 1 — a wallet with known coins
Create a descriptor wallet `coinsel_lab`. From `kisumu_miner`, send it exactly these five amounts
in **five separate transactions**: 0.10, 0.20, 0.30, 0.50, 1.00 BTC. Mine 1 block.
Record `COINSEL_WALLET=coinsel_lab`.

## Step 2 — predict (on paper, before any RPC)
At `fee_rate` = 10 sat/vB, a 1-input, 1-output P2WPKH tx has a vsize you computed in Stage 03.
1. What is the **largest** amount (in sat) the 0.30 BTC coin can pay **alone with no change**?
   → `A_MAX_AMOUNT_SATS`. (Fee = vsize × 10; the rest goes to the payee.)
2. BnB accepts a selection whose excess is at most `cost_of_change` (the cost of creating and later
   spending a change output). Pick an amount a little **below** your maximum that you predict is
   still paid by the 0.30 coin alone with no change → `A_AMOUNT_SATS`. Write how much excess you
   expect to go to the fee.
3. Payment B: 0.45 BTC. Will there be a change output? Which inputs might be used? Write it down.

## Step 3 — ask the wallet (without broadcasting)
Use `walletcreatefundedpsbt` with `fee_rate` = 10 and do **not** broadcast. Decode the result.
- Payment A at `A_AMOUNT_SATS`: record `A_INPUTS`, `A_HAS_CHANGE` (`true`/`false`), `A_FEE_SATS`.
- Payment A at `A_MAX_AMOUNT_SATS + 5`: what happens now? Explain in your journal.
- Payment B: record `B_INPUTS` and `B_HAS_CHANGE`.

## Step 4 — compare
In your journal: where was your prediction wrong? Which algorithm likely produced each result?
(SRD and Knapsack are randomised; run Payment B three times — does the choice change?)
