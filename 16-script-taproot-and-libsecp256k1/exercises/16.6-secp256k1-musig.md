## Part A — ECDSA by hand, tiny numbers (45 min)

On paper (or in a Python REPL you type yourself), use the toy curve y² = x³ + 7 over F₁₇
(it is *not* secure; it only has a handful of points). Find all points by brute force, pick a
generator, compute 2G and 3G with the group law, and do one ECDSA sign/verify with a nonce you choose.
Then sign two different messages with the **same** nonce and recover the private key with algebra.
Write the recovery formula.

<details><summary>Hint 1</summary>Two signatures (r, s1), (r, s2) on z1, z2 with the same k: subtract the two s equations.</details>
